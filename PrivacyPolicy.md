# Chernobot Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

Chernobot ("the app") is developed by Tim (SoupFork Studios / OldManBOMBIN). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## Short version

Chernobot does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It does not connect to the internet.

## What the app stores on your device

The app saves your progress (the current shift number, your score, which job notices you have already seen, and which weekly jobs are done) and your settings (such as left- or right-handed layout) in a small file in storage private to the app on your device. This information never leaves your device.

## Device sensors

The app uses your phone's motion sensors (gyroscope) so you can tilt the phone to look around the game. This sensor data is used only while you play, only on your device, and is not saved or transmitted.

## What the app does not do

- No account or sign-in is required.
- The app has no internet permission and sends no data anywhere.
- No location data is collected.
- The microphone and camera are not used.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs are used, and no ads are shown.
- No data is shared with advertisers or any third party.

## Deleting your data

Your saved progress can be erased with New Game in the app's menu, or by clearing the app's storage in Android's Settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
